Cutback waveguide of unplasticized Clear Flex 30 (poured 2025-10-17), imaged after removal from
the mold. The mold was not treated with mold release for this pour. The waveguide had torn from its Stretch Magic leads, so the laser was aimed into the
end of the waveguide at 30 to 45 degrees to the fiber axis. Light seen farther along the fiber is
therefore guided light rather than light shining on its surface.
