MICROSCOPY OF MOLDED CLEAR FLEX 30 WAVEGUIDES
Paper Figure 12 and Supplementary Figures S1 and S2

All images were taken with an Olympus DSX510 digital microscope. In the laser images, a red
diode laser was coupled into the waveguide so that light escaping from defects shows up. File
names are the names the images were exported with.
