The setup used two even 3-way splitters. A constant 5 cm path and a path of changing length, both
made of clear TPU fiber (MatterHackers), connected the first two ports of each splitter. The third
port was left open to bleed off some light, because otherwise too much light reached the
detector. A short piece of TPU (2-3 mm) was placed between one of the splitter connections and
the sensor's emitter or receiver.
